# Picture in Picture

Adds a Picture in picture button to video attachments in chat. Click it to open the video in a floating window, or click it again to return to Discord. The Loop video setting controls looping while it is floating.

The video must have loaded before it can enter picture in picture. This works with inline video attachments, not iframe embeds or voice-call screenshares.
